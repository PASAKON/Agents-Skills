## Framework lineage (concepts, not shipped prose)

Section 1 (improve-retention) adapts the **Fogg Behavior Model / Tiny Habits**
by BJ Fogg; Section 2 (hooked-ux) adapts the **Hook Model** by Nir Eyal. These
are widely-taught frameworks used here in MoonieX wording with forex/LuNar
examples. The source SKILL files are MIT (above); the underlying books are
credited as lineage and are not reproduced.
